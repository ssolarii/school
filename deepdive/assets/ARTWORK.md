# Artwork and type

The reef photograph is a real underwater photograph by Curt Storlazzi / USGS Pacific Coastal and Marine Science Center, published May 4, 2014. USGS marks it Public Domain. It is not presented as a Belize location.

Source: https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/coral-reef-1
Original: https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/WP_banner_image_NEW.jpg

The photograph is cropped and darkened in CSS; the original pixels are retained. The submersible and interface marks are code-native SVG, not generated imagery. The previous generated scene is no longer used.

Bitter and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next are locally hosted under the SIL Open Font License; licenses are in ../fonts/.
